Overview

One scan is all it takes

QRcode4you is pioneering the QR code revolution. We build smart, dynamic QR codes that help businesses, organisations and individuals connect, communicate and stay safe, making the world a more inclusive and connected place.

The range

One scan. Twelve smart solutions.

MyQR KNOCK

Let people reach you by one-way video call, free of charge, while your identity stays private.

MyQR HELP

Put any information behind a scan: websites, reviews, offers, campaigns or documents.

MyQR SOS

One scan sends a pre-written message, free of charge, to the emergency contact you choose.

MyQR NAVIGATE

Share your real-time GPS location and a safety check-in with loved ones, wherever you travel.

MyQR CONNECT

Instant, secure Wi-Fi access with a single scan. No passwords needed.

MyQR KNOW ME

Your business card on a smart QR code that saves your details straight into contacts.

MyQR IDENTITY

Identity data held in a secure digital space, readable only by a specially programmed scanner.

MyQR APPLIANCE

Every appliance’s brand, model, serial number, warranty and support links, one scan away.

MyQR BUZZ

Hassle-free delivery notifications for buildings without an intercom, with your privacy protected.

MyQR DONATE

Turn cash donations into seamless digital contributions for modern fundraising.

MyQR FIND PET

If your pet goes missing, a scan of its tag helps you find it.

MyQR PARKING

A mobile app for cashless, ticketless parking payments that also cuts running costs for car park operators.
