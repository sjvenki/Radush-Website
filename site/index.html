Capabilities

What we do

Five capabilities, one approach: technology that's built to move your business forward.

01

AI Enablement

Embed AI into the workflows you already run, or build a custom model when nothing off-the-shelf fits.

  • Put AI to work inside the workflows your teams already run. No rip-and-replace, no science projects.
  • Move from pilot to production. We build AI that ships, scales and earns its place, not demos that stall.
  • Turn your own data into a competitive advantage with models and agents tuned to your business, not generic tools.
  • Adopt AI with guardrails. Security, governance and cost controls are built in from day one.
02

Product Engineering

Build new products, or re-engineer existing ones, to be reliable and easy to evolve.

  • Get from idea to market faster. Lean, senior-led teams that ship working software in weeks, not quarters.
  • Build for scale from the first release. Architecture that holds up as your users and revenue grow.
  • Engineering quality you can measure. Automated testing and continuous delivery mean fewer defects and faster releases.
  • Give ageing products a second life. Re-engineer them to be easier to maintain, cheaper to run and ready to evolve.
03

Cloud & Application Modernisation

The right path for every application, chosen by business value, not habit, and without carrying old problems into the new environment.

  • Choose the right path for each application. We assess your portfolio against the 6Rs and decide what to rehost, replatform, refactor, repurchase, retire or retain, based on the business case.
  • Cut infrastructure and licensing costs while making your applications faster and more resilient.
  • Release in days, not months. Modern architecture frees your teams from legacy bottlenecks.
  • Modernise with zero drama. Phased, low-risk transitions that keep your business running throughout.
04

SRE

Digital reliability built on SRE: SLOs, error budgets and evidence of how work was done, in place of firefighting and ticket counts.

  • Digital reliability, not just ticket closure. Legacy service management tells you a ticket is closed. Digital reliability, built on SRE, shows how the work was done and what it went through.
  • Reliability you can put a number on. SLOs and error budgets turn uptime from a hope into a commitment.
  • Fewer incidents, faster recovery. Proactive monitoring and automation resolve issues before customers notice.
  • Treat reliability as a business capability, not an IT cost. It protects revenue, reputation and customer trust.
05

FinOps

Understand and control what your cloud actually costs, before the bill does.

  • See exactly where every cloud dollar goes. Full visibility by team, product and environment.
  • Cut cloud waste without cutting performance. Right-sizing and smart commitments free up budget for your next initiative.
  • Make cost a shared engineering and finance conversation, with forecasts and accountability built in.
  • Scale your cloud spend in line with business value, not ahead of it.
