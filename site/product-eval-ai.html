eVal.ai

AI-powered exam marking that scales to any number of papers.

Overview

Powering the future of exam evaluation

Every exam season, schools, colleges and universities have thousands of answer papers to evaluate manually, and students cannot get their results until every paper is done. This is slow and expensive, and the marks can vary from one examiner to another.

eVal.ai automates the evaluation. If a question has a model answer or a marking rubric, it scores the student’s answer against it. For open-ended questions, which a teacher would normally read and judge, it finds a reference answer, compares the student’s answer with it and gives a score. Teachers can spend their time teaching and coaching instead of marking.

The learner journey

A smarter path to a pass

01

First attempt

Every answer is marked, against a rubric or a researched reference.

02

Targeted retake

Students who fall short retake only the questions they got wrong, not the whole paper.

03

Narrowing down

Any question still missed comes back for another attempt, until only what matters is left.

04

Instructor coaching

If a student still falls short, they are referred to an instructor, so human time goes where it helps most.
