eC-hyre

From classroom to career, without the chaos of campus hiring.

Overview

Campus hiring, without the complexity

Every year, graduates leave campus ready to start their careers. And every year, placement teams and recruiters face the same tangle: coordinating hiring drives, handling hundreds of applications, juggling schedules and keeping everyone informed.

eC-hyre was built to take that weight off the table. It automates the campus hiring process from start to finish, so the move from graduate to professional is smooth, transparent and fast — for the students, the institution and the employers who want to hire them.

Who it's for

One process, three winners

01

Colleges & universities

Give your placement office one platform to run every hiring season with less manual effort and a clearer view of how it is going.

02

Students

A clearer, fairer path from final year to first job, so their passion and potential reach the right opportunity.

03

Recruiters

A simpler way to engage campuses and move from first interest to offer, without drowning in coordination.
