Overview

Our organisation

Technology consulting and engineering, built on DevOps and SRE foundations.

Radush was incorporated in 2022 with a vision of providing technology consulting and engineering services. Over time, it has evolved into an AI-driven solution provider and product re-engineering organisation — with AI now at the centre of much of what we build, from embedding intelligence into client workflows to the custom models and accelerators behind our own products. We use cutting-edge technology with highly skilled professionals, be it in custom-building a new product, re-engineering an existing product, or building a point solution for any industry-specific problem.

The products we engineer are built for minimal maintenance overheads, as we use a combination of DevOps and SRE methodologies for this. Consulting has been another line of ours, where we provide consolidation and optimisation services across tools, applications and infrastructure. We have an army of consultants with long industry experience who can quickly identify areas needing intervention and provide solutions.
